The Latest AI News

 

Claude Opus 5.5

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5. It says the model performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work and costs 40% less to run than Opus 5.

Why it matters for founders: If you build on AI models, a cheaper model can lower what it costs to run your product.

anthropic.com · Sep 22, 2026 →
 

Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

Anthropic's threat intelligence team says sophisticated attacks no longer require sophisticated attackers: criminal groups of any size can now run operations that once needed large specialist teams.

Why it matters for founders: Small teams hold customer data too, so basic security habits matter more than they used to.

anthropic.com · Sep 10, 2026 →
 

Claude discovers a novel enzyme system

Anthropic says Claude agents searched a large DNA sequence database for 21 hours and found an unfamiliar enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats. Its main function is still unknown and a preprint is out for review.

Why it matters for founders: A sign that AI agents are moving into lab science, not only software.

anthropic.com · Sep 23, 2026 →
 

How founders are rethinking capital in the AI era

BetaKit reports that AI is giving early-stage founders more room to experiment before raising significant amounts of capital, which is changing how they think about when to raise.

Why it matters for founders: Founders may be able to test an idea for longer before they need to raise money.

betakit.com · Sep 24, 2026 →
 

A Startup Idea to Think About

The one-page map

The idea in one line

A short guided interview that turns an owner's answers into a one-page map of how the business runs, with the slowest steps marked as candidates for AI.

Who needs it

Owner-operators of small trades and service businesses in Nova Scotia who have no time to document how their work flows.

Why now

AI has made small tools cheap to build, but owners say it feels abstract because nobody has drawn the map of how their work runs.

Try this in a week

Interview one owner for thirty minutes, draw the map by hand, and ask which step they would most like to stop doing.
